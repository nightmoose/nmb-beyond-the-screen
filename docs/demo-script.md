# Demo script — under three minutes

Record on a Fire TV if you can, otherwise the Fire TV / Vega simulator. The hackathon FAQ also allows an Android TV emulator. Keep the finished video under 3:00, public, in English, on YouTube or Vimeo.

Use the film’s own audio during the playback shot. Do not add a music bed. Do not add studio logos or posters. Bridge titles already use original art. The Sintel credit line must stay in frame while the trailer plays.

Arrow keys or the remote: move focus. Center / Enter: select. Back: leave a screen, or close the playback panel.

If Bedrock is configured, the home chip reads `Bedrock · …` and you can use the second version of the last beat. If it reads **Offline curated**, use the first version. Both are valid.

Spoken lines are about 340 words, roughly two and a half minutes. Leave a few seconds of quiet picture at the QR code and during playback.

## 0:00–0:12 — The room

**On screen:** App open on the home hero, *Master and Commander*. Chip visible in the corner.

**Say:** “Most TV apps answer a film with another film. Beyond the Screen answers it with the rest of the catalog: the book, the tool, the coat, the lesson.”

## 0:12–0:45 — The bridge

**Do:** Select **Beyond the screen** on the hero.

**On screen:** Category rail. Move down the rail: Naval history, Model ships, Nautical dress, Navigation. Stop on Navigation and move right until **Brass sextant** is focused. Hold on the QR code.

**Say:** “This is a bridge title. We don’t play the movie. We use the name, and we show what a person might actually go buy. Every card has one reason, and the code is an Amazon search. No affiliate tag, no scraped product page. You scan it from the couch.”

**Optional:** Step in, scan with a phone, and show the Amazon results for one second. Do not linger on other brands.

## 0:45–1:25 — Watch, then leave the film

**Do:** Back to home. On the **Plays in this app** shelf, open **Sintel**, then **Watch**. If you are short on time, use **Play Sintel** on the hero instead.

**On screen:** Trailer playing. The credit line stays up: Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0. Let the picture run for about eight seconds. Select **Beyond the screen**.

**Say:** “The films we can actually play are Creative Commons, from the Blender Foundation, and the credit stays on screen. Mid-playback, the same bridge opens. The trailer pauses. The recommendations sit next to the frame.”

**Do:** Move down two suggestions so the QR code changes. Back once. The trailer resumes.

## 1:25–1:50 — The other shelf

**Do:** Back to home. Move along **Bridge titles** just enough to show Pride and Prejudice, Interstellar, Whiplash, or Jiro. Open one for three seconds, then Back.

**Say:** “Ten titles ship in the demo. Six exist only as bridges. Four play. The layout is built for a remote: a rail, a row, and the phone on the right.”

## 1:50–2:25 — Offline, or Bedrock

**Offline version. On screen:** the **Offline curated** chip.

**Say:** “Nothing here requires a key. The pairings are curated so a judge can run the APK with no AWS account. If you add Bedrock, the same screen calls the Converse API, and a bad reply falls back to this set instead of failing empty.”

**Bedrock version. On screen:** the Bedrock chip, then a title whose status line says **Suggested with Amazon Bedrock**.

**Do:** Open any title that was not pre-cached if you want to show a short wait, then the Bedrock status line.

**Say:** “That chip means the television signed a Converse request to Amazon Bedrock. The model returns categories, a one-line why, and a search phrase. If the call fails, the curated set is what you still see.”

## 2:25–2:40 — Close

**On screen:** Home hero, or the sextant QR again.

**Say:** “The television starts the thought. The phone finishes the search. That’s the product: not another row of movies, a way off the screen and into the rest of the store.”

End before 3:00. Judges are not asked to watch past the mark.
